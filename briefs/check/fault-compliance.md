## FAULTS

### 1. ola-uniforma-deducere — SOLO, field `h1`
Exact text: `Pentru uniforma de serviciu plătești cu <em>£12</em> mai puțin impozit.`
Rule broken: **Rule 1, no guarantees** — promise of a saving.
Why: this is a flat indicative promise, not "poți" / "you can". It tells every reader who wears a work uniform that they pay £12 less tax. The post's own SCOPE contradicts it: a reader whose employer reimburses the cost gets nothing, a reader who paid no income tax that year gets nothing, self-employed readers are outside the scheme entirely, and the SCOPE states the cash value differs for Scottish and Welsh taxpayers. The other eight single-image headlines use the permitted "You can get £X" / "Poți" shape with the exclusion row carrying the qualification; this one asserts the saving as a certainty.

### 2. bham-disabled-facilities-grant — caption (main caption)
Exact text: `Households who assume they earn too much are often entitled to the full amount.`
Rules broken: **Rule 1, no guarantees** (promise of an outcome and of an amount) and **Rule 4, no invented statistics**.
Why: "often entitled to the full amount" asserts both a frequency and an entitlement to £30,000. Nothing in the post's SOURCES supports either; the two cited GOV.UK pages cover only the England maximum and the £6,000 savings level. The amount of any grant follows the occupational therapist's assessment of the works. The same caption contradicts this sentence seven lines later: `£30,000 is the maximum the law sets, not an entitlement.`

### 3. bham-disabled-facilities-grant — CARD 2, field `say`
Exact text: `Families who assume they earn too much often never ask.`
Rule broken: **Rule 4, no invented statistics**.
Why: a quantified claim about how often families fail to claim, on a card that ships as an image, with no source held for it.

### 4. ola-attendance-allowance — caption (main Romanian caption, opening line)
Exact text: `Attendance Allowance este cel mai puțin cerut ajutor pentru pensionari, și motivul este o neînțelegere: lumea crede că se verifică veniturile.`
Rule broken: **Rule 4, no invented statistics** (and CAP 3.7 / 3.33, unqualified superlative without evidence).
Why: "cel mai puțin cerut ajutor pentru pensionari" is a ranked statistical claim about take-up across all pensioner benefits. The post's SOURCES cover only the two weekly rates and the six-month condition. No take-up evidence is held.

### 5. ola-attendance-allowance — caption_li (opening line)
Exact text: `Attendance Allowance is the most under-claimed benefit for people over State Pension age, and the reason is a single misunderstanding: people assume it is means-tested.`
Rule broken: **Rule 4, no invented statistics** (and CAP 3.7 / 3.33).
Why: the English restatement of fault 4. Same superlative, same absence of evidence.

### 6. ola-plata-hmrc-timp — caption_li (opening line)
Exact text: `Every January a number of Self Assessment payments arrive late, and almost none of them were sent late.`
Rule broken: **Rule 4, no invented statistics**.
Why: "almost none of them were sent late" is a quantified claim about the proportion of late payments that were despatched on time. HMRC publishes no such figure and none is cited.

### 7. bham-priority-services-register — caption (main caption, opening line)
Exact text: `The Priority Services Register is free, and almost nobody is told about it.`
Rule broken: **Rule 4, no invented statistics**.
Why: "almost nobody is told about it" is a quantified claim about what energy suppliers and network operators do. The post's two Ofgem sources support only that the register is free extra help and that supplier and network operator keep separate registers.
