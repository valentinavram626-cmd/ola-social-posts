# Plain-reader check — cards-only.txt

Read by someone with no accounting or tax background, no sources, no explanations.
For each post: words I could not work out from the card, the sentence I had to read
twice, and what I actually took away.

## DID NOT UNDERSTAND

These are the posts where I could not reliably say what the card means.

### bham-sdlt-second-property-en
- **Did not understand:** "5 points to every band" — the whole headline. The card tells
  me it is "not the same as five per cent of the price" but never tells me what it *is*.
  Also: "band", "each slice of the price", "the day you complete", "stamp duty" itself.
- **Read twice:** "Each slice of the price has its own rate: 5% to £125,000, 7% to
  £250,000..." — I could not tell whether 5% applies to the first £125,000, or to the
  whole price if the price is under £125,000. "to" is doing too much work.
- **What I took from it:** Buying a second place costs extra on some tax, under £40,000
  it doesn't apply, and if you sell your old home in time you may get money back. I
  cannot tell you what I would pay or what "5 points" means in pounds.

### en-gift-aid-carry-back-en

  year's income", "box 8 on page TR 4", "your 2025-26 return", "the normal window for

- **Read twice:** "File without it and send a correction afterwards and HMRC cannot

- **What I took from it:** If I gave to charity I should put it on a form by 31 January

### ola-uniforma-deducere

- **What I took from it:** If I wear a work uniform I can ask HMRC for something worth
  about £12, going back four years. The "uneltele" (tools) in the closing line is the
  first I hear of tools — it isn't in the card above.

### en-starting-rate-savings
- **Did not understand:** What happens between the two numbers. Row 1 says £5,000 "in
  full below £12,570 of other income"; row 3 says excluded at "£17,570 or more". The
  £5,000-wide gap in the middle is unexplained, and "in full" hints something partial
  happens there without saying what. Also: what £12,570 *is*, and "HMRC works it out
  from your bank".
- **Read twice:** "[£5,000] in full below £12,570 of other income".
- **What I took from it:** If I earn very little I pay no tax on savings interest up to
  £5,000, and if I earn a lot I get nothing. If I'm in the middle, the card doesn't tell
  me.

### bham-sure-start-grant
- **Did not understand:** "[Excluded] most families with a child under 16". Read
  literally — which is all I can do — this says a maternity grant is not for families
  who already have children, which contradicts the card's own picture of someone
  expecting a baby. I cannot tell if I'm reading it wrong.
- **Read twice:** that exclusion row.
- **What I took from it:** Pregnant and on Universal Credit gets you £500, on form SF100
  signed by a midwife, within six months of the birth — **unless** you already have a
  child under 16, in which case apparently not.
