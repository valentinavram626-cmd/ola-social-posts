- Valentin videos: 218–340 views, 3–7 likes, occasional share.

**Reading of the numbers**
1. Volume is not the lever on Instagram: 14 text reels/day produce ~150 reach in total, less than one video of Valentin. People leave in the first 1–3 seconds → the first frame/hook is the failure point, not the topic.
2. TikTok rewards volume slightly (≈ 3,000 views/day from the batch) but does not convert: zero comments, almost zero likes → no signal to push videos beyond the test pool.
3. Topics that beat the median share a pattern: a trap or a loss ("60% effective tax", "£100 penalty", "payments on account scare") — i.e. stakes. Pure definitions ("what a VAT invoice must contain") sit at the floor.
4. Everything Valentin appears in wins by ~10×. The scarcest asset is his face and voice, and it is used once a day.
