# Daily post structure

Set by Valentin on 21 November 2026, replacing the carousel format. Live from
Monday 23 November 2026. Sunday 22 November still runs the previous format.

## The format

**One static image per post. No carousels anywhere.** The `solo` slide type in
`pipeline/carousel/` is the renderer. Nothing swipes, nothing animates.

## The two grids

**Instagram, Facebook and TikTok — on every brand — take 10 posts a day:**

| # | Slot label (RO / EN) | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Gândul zilei / Thought of the day | A motivational thought on business and work, an example of something Ola does well, or something genuinely good that happened in the UK in the last 24 hours |
| 2-4 | Vino la Ola Accountancy / Come to Ola Accountancy | Three warm invitations to move a limited company to us |
| 5-7 | Educație financiară cu Ola Accountancy / Financial education with Ola Accountancy | Three explaining one point each, written beautifully |
| 8 | Gluma zilei / Joke of the day | A joke that lands on a real accounting truth |
| 9 | Gluma serii / Joke of the evening | The second joke, under its own name |
| 10 | Citatul serii / Evening quote | A real quotation from a named philosopher, attributed |

**Every other connected network takes 5 a day**, drawn from the same material:

| # | Slot |
|---|---|
| 1 | Gândul zilei / Thought of the day |
| 2-3 | Vino la Ola Accountancy, two of the three |
| 4 | Gluma zilei, one of the two |
| 5 | Citatul serii / Evening quote |

That is Threads, LinkedIn, Bluesky and Pinterest.

**Google Business stays at ONE a day.** It is not raised to five. Five updates a
day on a local listing risks the profile being suspended, and the listing is what
brings telephone enquiries into Luton and Birmingham. Valentin confirmed this on
21 November 2026.

**Stories: all 10**, one per feed post, on the brands that have somewhere to put
them — Instagram and Facebook on `ola` and on `bham`. The `en` brand has only
TikTok connected and TikTok has no Story format, so it gets none, as before.

## Language

Each brand posts in the language it is configured for; this does not change.
`ola` is Romanian on Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and Threads, and English on
LinkedIn, Bluesky, Pinterest and Google Business. `bham` and `en` are English
only.

## Seasonal awareness

Every post is written against `content/seasonal-calendar.md`, which carries dated
windows for Christmas, New Year, Orthodox and Catholic Easter, Romanian national
days, UK bank holidays and the four seasons. A window opens days before the date
itself, so the content arrives while it is still useful.

The humour slots are where this shows most. A seasonal joke must tie the occasion
to a real accounting point — Father Christmas and the undeducted sleigh, not a
hat on a logo.

## The five slots, set by Valentin on 22 November 2026

He renamed all five and changed what three of them are for. His reasons are the rules.

**Gândul zilei** replaces the morning tax tip. It is motivational, about business and work, or a
concrete example of something Ola does well, or something genuinely good that happened in the UK in
the last 24 hours. The 24 hours are counted back from the moment the run STARTS, not from the moment
the post publishes, so the evening run can satisfy it. News must be verified at primary source and
must be genuinely positive; where there is none, the motivational or the Ola angle is always
available. This slot needs no figure.

**Vino la Ola Accountancy** replaces the Ltd-switching eyebrow, and the change is not only the name.
Valentin's words: the drafts opened on penalties, and "oamenii o sa interpreze ca sunt penalități pe
care le dam noi si noi vrem sa luam clienti, nu sa ii alungam." On our own account a penalty figure
in the opening line reads as though WE issue it. So an acquisition post opens on what the reader
gains by being with us — one fixed monthly fee, we handle the handover and write the professional
clearance letter, the free 30 minutes, someone who answers. A deadline may appear in the body as
reassurance, never as the opening threat, and no penalty figure goes in the hero or the punch.

**Educație financiară cu Ola Accountancy** replaces the plain educational slot. Every figure stays
accurate and every exclusion stays present, but the wording was "prea directe" — write the way a
good teacher explains something to someone they like, a sentence that opens a door rather than a
line off a form. Warmth and rhythm, varied sentence lengths, still no jargon.

**Gluma zilei and Gluma serii** are two different names, because both jokes carried the same one and
a reader would think the post had repeated. And they must actually be funny: nobody understood the
sleigh joke. A joke works when the reader recognises themselves or someone they know, in one beat,
with no accounting knowledge needed to get the laugh; the accounting point lands underneath. A joke
that needs explaining is replaced, not explained.

**Citatul serii** replaces our own evening reflection with a real quotation from a named philosopher,
the name carried on the frame. Only quotations whose wording and author are certain — misattribution
is everywhere. Prefer authors long dead and in the public domain, and choose lines about work, time,
patience, discipline, money or building something, so the quotation belongs on an accountancy firm's
account rather than being generic inspiration.

## How every piece is written

Follow the `ola-seed-of-truth` structure: open on something real and verifiable
that the reader recognises, let them see themselves in it, turn it into the
consequence that affects them, then say plainly what Ola does. The persuasive
force comes from the facts being true, so an unsourceable opening is replaced,
never dressed up. Invented figures, fabricated client stories, false urgency and
guarantees are out, as they always were.

## Two writing rules added on 21 November 2026

From reviewing what the department actually published in the week to 21
November, against what the platforms reward.

### The first line may not lean on a name the reader does not know

Posts have been opening on the name of the scheme. A reader who has never heard
of it has no reason to stop, and most of the people a post reaches have never
heard of it. The name is the answer, not the hook.

The first line carries the figure and what the reader gets or loses. The scheme
is named in the body, where it becomes useful rather than a barrier.

> Not: "NHS Healthy Start. GBP 4.65 a week from the 10th week of pregnancy."
> But: "GBP 9.30 a week for every child under one, and most parents never claim it."

The test: show the first line alone to someone who has never heard of the
scheme. If it means nothing to them, it is a heading, not a hook. Applies to the
frame, the caption's opening line and the TikTok title.

### Ask for the save, in the caption, never on the frame

Saves and shares expand reach further than likes do, and this firm's content is
unusually worth saving: amounts, thresholds and deadlines are reference material
a reader wants back later. Nothing has been asking for it.

One line in the caption, immediately before the general-information disclaimer.
Vary the wording; the same sentence every day stops registering. Ask for a save
when the post carries a figure or date the reader will need again, a share when
the post is clearly about somebody other than the reader. Never both in one
caption, and never ask for a like, which is the weakest of the three and spends
the only ask available.

### What is deliberately not adopted

The Instagram growth advice in circulation optimises for reach in the millions.
This firm does not need reach, it needs limited company owners in Luton and
Birmingham. Do not chase reach at the cost of the audience, do not adopt a
provocative tone to win it, and never raise posting volume for it. Instagram
restricted activity on the Birmingham account on 21 November at the volume
already running.

The reels pipeline stays off. Valentin decided this on 21 November 2026.
