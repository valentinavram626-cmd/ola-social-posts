# Daily post structure

Set by Valentin on 21 November 2026, replacing the carousel format. Live from
Monday 23 November 2026. Sunday 22 November still runs the previous format.

## The format

**One static image per post. No carousels anywhere.** The `solo` slide type in
`pipeline/carousel/` is the renderer. Nothing swipes, nothing animates.

## The two grids

**Instagram, Facebook and TikTok — on every brand — take 10 posts a day:**

| # | Slot | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Morning tip | One verified, useful thing, posted first thing |
| 2-4 | Acquisition | Three in the style of the live Meta ad: the Ltd-switching offer |
| 5-7 | Educational | Three explaining one accounting point each |
| 8-9 | Humour | Two jokes that land on a real accounting truth |
| 10 | Evening thought | One reflection on business and life, written by us |

**Every other connected network takes 5 a day**, drawn from the same material:

| # | Slot |
|---|---|
| 1 | Morning tip |
| 2-3 | Acquisition, two of the three |
| 4 | Humour, one of the two |
| 5 | Evening thought |

That is Threads, LinkedIn, Bluesky and Pinterest.

**Google Business stays at ONE a day.** It is not raised to five. Five updates a
day on a local listing risks the profile being suspended, and the listing is what
brings telephone enquiries into Luton and Birmingham. Valentin confirmed this on
21 November 2026.

**Stories: all 10**, one per feed post, on the brands that have somewhere to put
them — Instagram and Facebook on `ola` and on `bham`. The `en` brand has only
TikTok connected and TikTok has no Story format, so it gets none, as before.

## Language

Each brand posts in the language it is configured for; this does not change.
`ola` is Romanian on Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and Threads, and English on
LinkedIn, Bluesky, Pinterest and Google Business. `bham` and `en` are English
only.

## Seasonal awareness

Every post is written against `content/seasonal-calendar.md`, which carries dated
windows for Christmas, New Year, Orthodox and Catholic Easter, Romanian national
days, UK bank holidays and the four seasons. A window opens days before the date
itself, so the content arrives while it is still useful.

The humour slots are where this shows most. A seasonal joke must tie the occasion
to a real accounting point — Father Christmas and the undeducted sleigh, not a
hat on a logo.

## How every piece is written

Follow the `ola-seed-of-truth` structure: open on something real and verifiable
that the reader recognises, let them see themselves in it, turn it into the
consequence that affects them, then say plainly what Ola does. The persuasive
force comes from the facts being true, so an unsourceable opening is replaced,
never dressed up. Invented figures, fabricated client stories, false urgency and
guarantees are out, as they always were.
